# attacker_scripts — your payloads and scripts (optional but encouraged)

Put the inputs, requests, or small scripts you used here so your work is
reproducible. This is optional for grading but good practice and helps your
instructor verify your findings.

Examples you might add:
- `sqli_login.txt` — the exact payload you put in the login email field.
- `sqli_search.txt` — your UNION-based search injection.
- `xss_reflected.txt` / `xss_stored.txt` — your XSS payloads.
- Any `.py` / `curl` script that reproduces the request.

Keep everything local to your own Juice Shop instance — authorized testing only.
